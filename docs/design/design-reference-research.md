# Design Reference Research

Status: completed research summary
Basis: `main` after design research brief
Purpose: document visual reference research and define the direction for RADJA AC UI Batch 1.

## Executive summary

The recommended visual direction for RADJA AC is:

- modern, clean, and trust-first,
- mobile-first,
- conversion-focused around WhatsApp consultation,
- using real RADJA AC showroom, stock, delivery, display, and installation assets,
- simple enough for local business visitors to understand quickly,
- stronger than the old live visual presentation without copying competitors.

The website should not be rebuilt as a flashy portfolio site. It should feel like a reliable AC sales and consultation business: clear offer, real proof, easy navigation, and strong WhatsApp CTA.

## RADJA AC design principle

RADJA AC should lead with:

1. what it sells: original multi-brand AC units,
2. how it helps: PK consultation, stock check, delivery, installation options, and multi-unit procurement,
3. why it can be trusted: real showroom/stock/display/delivery assets from Pamijen, Sokaraja, Banyumas,
4. how visitors convert: chat WhatsApp first, not instant checkout.

## Patterns to adopt

### 1. Sticky navbar with clear CTA

Navbar should be simple, sticky, and conversion-oriented.

Recommended desktop items:

- Jual AC
- Katalog
- Brand
- Pengadaan
- Bukti Pengiriman
- Kontak
- WhatsApp CTA

Recommended mobile behavior:

- compact logo,
- menu button,
- visible WhatsApp CTA,
- simple full-screen or dropdown menu.

### 2. Hero that explains the business immediately

The hero should answer three questions in the first screen:

- What does RADJA AC sell?
- Why should visitors trust it?
- What should visitors do next?

Recommended hero message:

- original multi-brand AC,
- consultation before buying,
- PK, stock, delivery, and installation option checks,
- WhatsApp as the main action.

### 3. Use real photos as proof, not decoration

RADJA AC has valid physical assets from Pamijen, Sokaraja, Banyumas. These should be used as proof:

- showroom photo,
- stock/gudang photo,
- delivery photo,
- product display photo,
- installation/procurement proof photo.

The design should avoid generic stock-photo energy.

### 4. Trust proof above the fold

Early trust signals should include:

- showroom / stock can be checked,
- multi-brand options,
- consult PK before buying,
- payment after everything is clear,
- delivery and installation options by confirmation,
- physical base in Pamijen, Sokaraja, Banyumas.

### 5. Category cards should guide users

Homepage should quickly route visitors into:

- AC 1/2 PK,
- AC split rumah,
- AC low watt,
- AC inverter,
- AC kantor/komersial,
- pengadaan AC banyak unit.

### 6. One primary conversion path

The main CTA is WhatsApp. Secondary actions can exist, but should not compete too strongly.

Recommended primary CTA:

- Chat WhatsApp
- Konsultasi PK & cek stok

Recommended secondary CTA:

- Lihat katalog
- Lihat bukti pengiriman

## Patterns to avoid

- overly artistic layout that reduces clarity,
- too many CTAs in the hero,
- fake urgency or fake discount banners,
- hardcoded fixed prices,
- unsupported "termurah" claims,
- unsupported "ready semua" claims,
- unsupported "dealer resmi" claims,
- fake branch/location claims,
- stock-photo-heavy design,
- heavy animation,
- cluttered mobile header,
- copying competitor layout pixel-for-pixel.

## Recommended visual direction

### Style

Use a clean, premium local-business style:

- white/light background,
- strong dark text,
- blue/navy for trust and structure,
- warm accent color for CTA,
- rounded cards,
- high contrast buttons,
- real-photo hero,
- clean section spacing.

### Tone

The website should feel:

- professional,
- helpful,
- local and real,
- practical,
- not pushy,
- not fake luxury,
- not marketplace-style clutter.

### CTA language

Recommended CTA text:

- Chat WhatsApp
- Konsultasi PK & cek stok
- Tanya stok hari ini
- Kirim ukuran ruangan
- Lihat katalog AC

Avoid:

- Beli sekarang jika it implies instant checkout,
- Harga termurah,
- Semua ready,
- Promo besar tanpa source.

## Proposed homepage structure

1. Sticky navbar
   Logo, menu, WhatsApp CTA.

2. Hero section
   Real showroom/stock photo, headline, short explanation, CTA WhatsApp, secondary catalog/proof link.

3. Trust strip
   3 to 4 compact proof points:
   - Konsultasi PK dulu
   - Cek stok sebelum deal
   - Pengiriman unit
   - Opsi pemasangan by confirmation

4. Brand proof / multi-brand section
   Brand logos and explanation that final stock/type is confirmed by admin.

5. Category section
   Cards for AC 1/2 PK, split rumah, low watt, inverter, commercial, procurement.

6. Proof photo section
   Showroom, stock, delivery, display photos with careful captions.

7. Process section
   Kirim kebutuhan, admin cek PK/stok, konfirmasi harga/pengiriman/pasang, deal.

8. CTA section
   Strong WhatsApp consultation block.

9. Footer
   Contact, location, important pages, route links.

## UI Batch 1 scope

UI Batch 1 should stay small and safe.

Included:

- navbar redesign,
- homepage hero redesign,
- primary WhatsApp CTA,
- secondary catalog/proof CTA,
- early trust strip under hero,
- use structured assets from `public/`,
- no route changes,
- no redirect changes,
- no sitemap/robots changes,
- no technical SEO changes.

Excluded from UI Batch 1:

- full homepage redesign,
- all money page redesign,
- article layout redesign,
- new pricing tables,
- new claims,
- custom domain cutover.
